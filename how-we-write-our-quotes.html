How we write our quotes

Every quote on Strong Mom Quotes is written by us. We do not copy lines from other websites, and we never attach a real person's name to words they did not say. That is what makes each quote safe for you to copy, print on a card or a mug, or use in something you sell.

Original, not collected

These are not scraped from Pinterest boards or quote databases. Each line is written for a specific moment, a tired morning, a proud milestone, a hard season, so it sounds like something a real mother or father would actually say, rather than a polished slogan.

Never falsely attributed

Most quote sites hang lines on famous names to make them look weightier, and a lot of those attributions are simply wrong. We do not do that. Our quotes carry only the name Strong Mom Quotes, which means you are never unknowingly printing a misquote.

Checked before it goes live

Before a collection is published we read every line for tone and honesty, remove anything that reads as toxic positivity, and check that no two pages lean on the same wording. Affirmations are written to steady you on a hard day, not to pretend the hard parts away.

The one exception, clearly marked

Our classic mom quotes page is the single place we quote real, named authors, and only genuine public-domain sources, with the author shown. Everywhere else on the site, the words are ours.

Why this matters for you

Because the words are original and unattributed, you can print them, frame them, or put them on something you sell without a licensing worry. Open any quote in the free card maker and it is ready to share.
